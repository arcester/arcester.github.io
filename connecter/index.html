Privacy

Connecter works on your Mac and with your network. Your data stays yours.

  • No data collection. Connecter does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data with the developer or with third parties.
  • Credentials in the Keychain. Passwords are stored exclusively in the macOS Keychain and are never written to disk in plain text.
  • Local data. Settings and the activity log are kept only on your Mac, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • App Sandbox. The app runs isolated inside the macOS App Sandbox, with access only to what it needs.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before installing it.

What kind of shares does it support?

Shares over SMB, the protocol used by most NAS devices (Synology, QNAP…), Windows servers and other Macs.

Which version of macOS do I need?

macOS 26 Tahoe or later.

What happens if I close the lid or switch Wi‑Fi networks?

When your Mac wakes up or the network changes, Connecter mounts your shares again automatically. If there’s no connection, it pauses until the network comes back.

Where are my passwords stored?

Only in the macOS Keychain, the same system macOS itself uses for your passwords.
